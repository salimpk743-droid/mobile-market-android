App name: Mobile Market
Short description (80 chars):
Buy & sell used phones across Pakistan — PTA, city, photos.

Full description:
Mobile Market is the used-phone classifieds app for Pakistan.

Search iPhone, Samsung, Xiaomi, Vivo, Oppo and more. Filter by brand, memory, PTA approved or non-PTA, and 200+ cities from Lahore and Karachi to smaller towns.

Post an ad with photos, price, city and area. Edit the price later, mark it sold, relist, or delete it from My Ads.

The app shows the same live marketplace as the website, with the same listings and seller tools.
